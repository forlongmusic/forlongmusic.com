about

forlong is looping, poetic bedroom pop / laptop twee / indietronica made by Monica & Ezra.

Monica is a songwriter, producer, and guitarist operating in Brooklyn, NY. Songs off the debut EP Yesterday's Clothes capture her evolution in each capacity since she began working on the project in 2024. Ezra is a songwriter, producer, engineer, and multi-instrumentalist. He lends his expressive drum programming and creative rhythm guitar to the tracks. The two met in 2016 and have been together for 10 years.

Yesterday's Clothes was written, recorded, produced, and mixed in their home studio. The two work on other projects out of Secret Beach Studios in Williamsburg, Brooklyn.
